GOOD TO KNOW

A few helpful answers.

A little clarity before you shop.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery is estimated at 14 days after dispatch. Timing varies by destination. Delivery charges and final terms will be shown before online orders open.

Can I ask about a product or colour?

Of course. Send the product name and the option you’re interested in through WhatsApp or our on-site chat. Please don’t send payment card details.

What happens if you’re offline?

Your message is saved in the store’s inbox. You can reopen the chat on this browser to see our reply. An online indicator appears only while the owner is available in the dashboard.

Can I contact you by email?

Email support will be added here later. For now, WhatsApp and on-site chat are the best ways to reach us.
